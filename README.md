# The Last Sleeper

*Последний спящий* is a first-person survival horror game set in a flooded hospital. Sprint through a ward and the noise can draw attention. Creep past the beds and you might reach a dry place to sleep. Once asleep, you can explore the hospital's other world.

In a fight, you weave images into spells. You can use the same image more than once and combine up to four. A mirror door sends an enemy elsewhere on the floor. A cuckoo strikes after two turns, but the whole floor hears it. The title screen lets you choose between turn-based and real-time combat.

## Play

Download [last-sleeper.html](./last-sleeper.html) and open it in a desktop browser with WebGL enabled. The game and its assets are bundled into that one file. The interface is in Russian. Choose **Новый забег** (New run) to begin or **Управление** (Controls) to open the in-game guide.

## Controls

| Action | Exploration | Turn-based combat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move or select a target | WASD to move, Shift to sprint, C to sneak | A / D to change target |
| Weave a spell | 1–8 to add an image | 1–9 to add a thought from your hand |
| Cast | Left click | Enter or left click |
| Undo | X or right click to clear the weave | X or right click to remove the last thought |
| Use an item | Scroll to select, Q to use | Scroll to select, Q to use your turn |

Press **E** to interact with the ward, **Z** to try to sleep and **I** to open your pockets. In a turn-based encounter, **F** lets you try to escape.
